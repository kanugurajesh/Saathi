Receivables & daily WhatsApp summary

Money owed to the hospital by every payer, grouped by how long it has been unpaid, with deductions and denials. The owner gets it as a WhatsApp summary every morning instead of finding out at month-end.

Where each number comes from. Private TPA claims update on their own: the portal bots pick them up at discharge. Aarogyasri, EHS and PM-JAY don't. The bots do not log into those government portals, so their claims come from the CSV export the billing desk uploads, or from manual entry. Each payer shows when its data was last refreshed, and the WhatsApp summary warns when a payer's figures are stale.
